What to have ready before the visit

A little preparation can make it easier to explain the situation and give the plumber room to work. Consider this short checklist:

  • Note which fixture, drain, or appliance is affected.
  • Describe when the problem began and whether it comes and goes.
  • Mention any earlier repairs or service related to the same spot, if you know them.
  • Clear nearby items if they block access to the area.
  • Keep children and pets away from the immediate work space.

A plumber may need to inspect the area before explaining the repair options. Ask what the inspection shows and what work is being proposed. It is reasonable to request a plain explanation before deciding how to proceed.
